Galvanic Chains

The ways through Theurgy to the Galvanic Essence, for planning the energy challenge's quest chapters (#209, #175). There is no one right way: a team takes whichever its resources allow. The Galvanic Essence is at the top. Choose how to make it, and what that recipe needs appears below it; keep choosing until every branch reaches a raw or refined item, such as an ingot or a raw ore, where the Chain starts (getting those out of essence is the Recycler's job, not the challenge's). Only the last step, the one that makes the Galvanic Essence, is a new recipe; every other is Theurgy's own. The panel on the right shows what your route costs, per Galvanic Essence.

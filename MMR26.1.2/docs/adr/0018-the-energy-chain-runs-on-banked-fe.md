# The energy chain runs on banked FE

The energy challenge's chain was Theurgy's apparatus as Theurgy ships it: heated by Pyromantic Braziers burning furnace fuel, its mercury flux burned from shards and crystals, and its Galvanic Essence incubated from one mercury crystal, one mineral salt and one redstone sulfur. A playtester took it against the Improved tier, set it up in half an hour, and hand-fed 8 Galvanic Essences in a minute from crystals crafted out of the Basic mix's shards, with no FE spent at all. The handicap, which stops generators, cost nothing, while money's mint, run on FE, took hours to build. Now FE is the chain's fuel: the Mercury Catalyst draws FE for the mercury flux it makes, and while the team plays energy a Brazier gives the chain no heat, so every heated stage and every reformation is paid from FE banked ahead. The Galvanic Essence takes many more inputs: mercury, salt and sulfur are each packed into a Galvanic Charge in Assemblers, salt and sulfur climb reformation stages on the pedestals first, the Incubator makes a Galvanic Core from the three charges, and Assemblers make the Galvanic Essence from several cores.

## Considered options

- **An FE-powered last step**, a Craftworks machine finishing the Galvanic Essence. FE is spent only at the end, on a handful of items, so it stays cheap.
- **FE-powered steps along the way**, Craftworks machines between Theurgy's stages. Part of what was chosen, through the Galvanic Charges, but on its own it leaves Theurgy's own stages free.
- **A mercury only the chain makes.** Theurgy has none: the Distiller is the only source of shards, and crystals are crafted from them.
- **The Basic mix losing its mercury shard.** Kept: a saved-up shard still has to be packed into a charge, under the handicap, on banked FE.
- **Saved-up stock not counting.** Overturns ADR 0007's rule that a lock stops a chain being run ahead, not a stock being saved up.

## Consequences

- The Brazier heats again once the challenge ends, by a win or a stop, so a won chain runs on furnace fuel. A reset doesn't end it: the handicap stays, and the Brazier stays cold through it.
- The Galvanic Charge and Galvanic Core recipes are chain recipes, locked until the start (ADR 0007), so packing saved-up shards waits for the handicap.
- The FE each unit of flux costs is a server setting, tuned in playtests until the chain at Improved takes as long to build and run as money's mint.
- Extraction lets a team that won other challenges first feed the chain's lowest stages from their Transmuted Essence, at the cost of not selling it.

# A challenge's mods take only raw and refined items

A challenge's mods, Theurgy for the energy challenge and the mods of the challenges to come, take only raw and refined items: ores, raw ores, ingots, dusts and the like, never an intermediate. What an essence decomposes into is brought down to those bands on the Recycler, which is what it is for, and the challenge's Chain starts there. The pack writes no recipe that feeds an intermediate into a challenge's mod; the one recipe it adds to a challenge's mods is the last step, which makes the challenge's Transmuted Essence from that mod's own products.

We could have scripted recipes that take Advanced's intermediates (a piston, a copper bulb) straight into Theurgy, as the first research for #209 proposed. That made each challenge's start a set of pack recipes, tied to whatever an essence's mix held, and skipped the Recycler. Keeping the challenge's mods on their own recipes, from the bands they already take, keeps the recycling where it belongs and lets a mix change without touching any challenge.

## Consequences

- An essence's mix has to recycle down to the raw and refined items its challenge's Chain needs.
- Recycling is no part of a Chain: a Chain's costs are counted from raw and refined items, not from essence.

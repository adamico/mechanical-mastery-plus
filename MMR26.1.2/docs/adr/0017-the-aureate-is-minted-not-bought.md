# The Aureate is minted, not bought

The money challenge's chain was the Shop's Offers alone: sell, then buy the Aureate Essence. A playtester banked gold blocks before starting, sold them under the handicap and filled the quota in minutes, since a chain of Offers has nothing to build once the Permit is given. Now the Aureate is made, as every other Transmuted Essence is, by a chain the pack writes on the suite's machines, protection's way: ingots melted with lava, cast in sand molds, cooled, and parted into coins, coins of all three metals sealed into an Aureate. The Shop no longer sells the Aureate and buys it as it buys the other three, for more than its lava and metal cost but less than twice that, so it sells at a profit at full price and at a loss at the half the money handicap pays, and a mint never pays for itself while the challenge is played; it never buys a coin. Money stays the heart of the chain through what it buys, above all the lava every melt takes, which only the Shop sells, so the team runs a sales line to feed a mint.

## Considered options

- **Saturation**: the Shop paying less for a good sold lately, as the Diet feeds less. A price that falls still lets a Seller burn its goods for nothing, and one that forgets by count, as the Diet does, is beaten by selling banked goods in turn.
- **A per-good allowance**, a Seller holding back a good once the Shop has bought its fill. It fixes banking at the Shop, not at the chain, and leaves money's chain with nothing to build.
- **Only goods made after the start sell under the handicap.** Tracking every item's origin, and it rules out preparing, which ADR 0009 keeps as a strategy.
- **The Aureate's price rising with each one bought.** Still a finite sum, so still bankable.

## Consequences

- Banked gold now helps the way banked FE does (ADR 0009): it feeds the mint, but the mint is locked until the start (ADR 0007) and is built and run under C.O.D.'s clock.
- Gold is either sold or minted, never both, which is the choice the handicap sharpens.
- A team short of a metal exchanges coins up for it, so a tier whose mix holds little gold is played from copper.
- Lava is spent by every melt and only the Shop gives it: nothing in the pack makes lava, no lava farm undercuts the Shop, and the Shop never buys it back.
- The Galvanic, Warding and Vital Essences, whose sale hangs on the money Permit, are priced by the same rule, more than their inputs cost and less than twice that, so a won chain doesn't pay for the mint outright.

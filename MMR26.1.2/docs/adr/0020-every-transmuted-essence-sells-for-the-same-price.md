# Every Transmuted Essence sells for the same price

The Shop bought each Transmuted Essence for more than its inputs cost but less than twice that, so a won chain's extras were a small profit at full price and a loss at the share the money handicap pays (ADR 0017). Priced on inputs alone, an essence was worth almost nothing: once the Shop sold ores only as ore blocks and crude oil as a consumable, the Galvanic's inputs came to about a dollar and the Vital's to nothing, though each chain takes a player a long time to set up. Now the Shop buys every Transmuted Essence for the same $2,000, which pays for the setup, not the inputs: a chain, once built, runs untouched until the team scales it. Since the challenges are taken in any order, no essence is a better sale than another; what keeps them even is their chains, tuned to take a comparable setup, machines and logistics both, not their prices (#337).

## Considered options

- **Keep the cap at twice the inputs.** An essence then sells for cents, and a won chain is not worth running.
- **A multiple of the inputs.** It follows what a chain spends, not what it takes to build: the Vital, whose inputs cost nothing, sold for nothing.
- **A price per block of setup.** It follows the setup, but makes one challenge a better sale than another, and every recipe change reprices an essence.

## Consequences

- A won chain's extras are a real income, even at the money handicap's share, so a won chain can pay for the next challenge, money's among them; the mint can pay for itself while the money challenge is played.
- Each chain's recipes, rates and processing times are what balance the four, and a chain far simpler or harder to set up than the others is tuned, not repriced.
- The price stays above each essence's inputs at Shop prices, so selling one is never a loss at full price.

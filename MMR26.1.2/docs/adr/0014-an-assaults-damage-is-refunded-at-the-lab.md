# An Assault's damage is refunded at the lab

What an Assault's mobs break on a team's island drops nothing and is recorded, and when the Assault ends, by a win, a reset or a stop, the team's lab holds it all as the team's refund: each block as if a member had mined it with silk touch, and each hanging entity with what it held. Nothing is put back in the world. An Assault runs for a quarter of an hour or more while the factory must keep decomposing, so a team rebuilds what it loses as the fight goes on; a refund gives back what was broken without undoing any of that, and so the damage hurts during the fight, as ADR 0010 means it to, and leaves nothing lasting.

## Considered options

- **Put each block back in its spot.** Every rule for a spot the team has built over since went wrong: leaving it lost the broken block for good, since it dropped nothing, and punished a team for patching a breach; overwriting it undid the team's own rebuilding; and a spot broken twice needed a rule of its own.
- **Spots no one may build on until the end, then filled.** A destroyed machine couldn't be rebuilt in place for the rest of the fight while Inflation climbs.
- **Blasts that drop everything where they break.** On a skyblock the drops fall into the void, the next blast destroys them, and the team must run into the fight to collect them.
- **A refund at each breather too.** A team could wait out a step, and ADR 0010's threat to the base would be for show.
- **A snapshot of the island, blasts that break nothing, or no repair at all**, rejected in #254's triage.

## Consequences

- Only the Assault's own mobs count: a creeper blast, and every block that pops off or breaks because a recorded one is gone. The team's own explosions don't, nor does a home gate, which is played after the handicap is gone; sand or gravel a blast sets falling lands as a block, so it isn't counted either.
- The Assault's endermen never pick up blocks, and its blazes' fireballs never set blocks alight, so blasts are the one way it damages the base.
- A machine whose mod keeps its contents when mined keeps them in its refund; one that spills its inventory when mined has that inventory added to the refund.
- The refund waits at the lab across restarts, with no cap and no expiry, and a later Assault's adds to it. A member takes it from the lab's menu, or the lab pushes it into any block beside it that takes items, a stack at a time while the island is loaded; nothing can pull it out.
- When the Assault ends the team's members online are told what waits at the lab, and a member logging in is told while any of it is left.

# The protection handicap is an Assault, not stronger mobs

Under the protection handicap, the companion mod sends an Assault at the playing team's island: endless Gateways to Eternity gates on platforms it builds in the void, with unbreakable bridges to the island, whose waves grow harder step by step, with a breather before each step, and don't stop until the quota is filled or Inflation reaches its cap. A gate that fails opens again at the wave it had reached. The waves are the handicap; no mob is made stronger on its own. The Assault's mobs, and a home gate's, alone drop Rift Shards, which the Warding Essence is made from, so a team can save ordinary drops ahead but never the quota.

We could have done what #211 first read: more natural spawns on the island, attribute buffs, special variants, with Gateways' gates a separate thing the team opened. On a skyblock few mobs spawn and fewer can reach the island, so buffing them costs a team little, and the gates the team opened would have been a farm it ran at its own pace. Making the gates the handicap puts the challenge where the team lives, and makes the factory's Defences and the team's gear what wins it.

## Consequences

- The Assault ends on a win or a reset: its gates, platforms, bridges and remaining mobs are removed, and the team keeps its unused Rift Shards.
- Its mobs hunt the team's players, so its gates close while no member is on the island, and open again at the wave they had reached when one comes back. Inflation goes on.
- Explosions damage the playing team's island, claimed or not: the companion mod turns on the team's FTB Chunks `allow_explosions` while the Assault runs, turns it back on if a player turns it off, and restores the team's own value when it ends. Every other claim protection stays.
- A mob knocked off a bridge dies in the void with its drop.
- A won chain goes on making Warding Essence through the home gate, which drops fewer Rift Shards than the Assault.
- Defences are free from the start and always fed by the factory, so growing them ahead is a strategy, as banking FE is (ADR 0009).

# A challenge's chain is locked until the team starts it

A challenge's chain, the recipes of its mods' machines and of what feeds them, stays locked for a team until the team starts that challenge at the lab, and stays unlocked from then on, past a win or a reset. Locking is per recipe, not per mod: what a challenge's mods offer outside the chain, as AdminShop's Shop, is never locked, and a locked mod's items can still come from an essence's mix, to be recycled, as Neo Vitae's Rune of Sacrifice II comes from Advanced's.

We could have left a challenge's mods free from the start, as Theurgy and Neo Vitae were in 0.0.1. A team could then build a whole chain while no handicap applies and C.O.D. makes no Inflation, and starting the challenge would only switch a finished factory on. The handicap and the Inflation clock are the challenge, so the chain has to be built under them.

## Consequences

- Theurgy's chain, free in 0.0.1, has to be locked behind the energy challenge.
- A reset keeps the chain unlocked: the factory a failed attempt built is what it earned, and a second attempt starts with it.
- A chain unlocked by one challenge stays open for the team's later challenges.
- Gearworks is no challenge's mod, since locking it would lock the suite's own gear; it stays free, as the gear that protection makes worth having.
- A lock stops a chain being built ahead, not a stock being saved up. What a handicap acts on, a team's money and FE, is set aside while the challenge is played (glossary **Handicap**), so the money challenge's chain is paid only with money earned during it.

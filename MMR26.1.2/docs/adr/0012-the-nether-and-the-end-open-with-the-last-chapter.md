# The Nether and the End open with the last chapter

A team reaches the Nether and the End only once it has won all four challenges. They are the last chapter's places: Quintessence's Voidworks way runs on motes harvested there, by displacers that work only in the Nether or only in the End, and the End's dragon gives the breath the draconic siphon takes. An obsidian frame forms a Nether portal only on the island of a team that has won all four, and never off a team's island in the Overworld; in the Nether a frame always forms, so the way home is there. The End is reached by an End portal the team builds from crafted End Portal Frames, whose recipe a team unlocks with its fourth win.

Neither is vanilla, and neither is the void the Overworld is. The Nether is a sea of lava with floating fortresses and nothing else, so the Nether displacer has lava to drink and the fortresses give blaze rods, nether wart and wither skeletons. The End is a plain of end stone with its main island on it, its dragon, the dragon's towers, End cities and chorus, and the void only above it, so a team builds upwards.

We could have removed both, since nothing before the last chapter needs them and the Assault and Theurgy give their drops, but that would have cost Voidworks its displacers and the dragon its breath. We could have left them free from the start, but a team could then build Quintessence's chain ahead of its four wins, which ADR 0007 rules out for every chain. We first kept them void around their islands, but the void Nether had no lava for its displacer and nothing only it gives. Vanilla generation would have given them back, but also every one of Voidworks' gate items for free, bastions' netherite and crying obsidian above all, and taken the theme with it.

## Consequences

- The lock is on the portal forming, by the island's team, not on travel: a player whose team has not won all four may go through a finished team's portal. It never closes again, since a won challenge stays won.
- End Portal Frames can't be had in survival, so the crafted frame is the only way into the End. It is crafted on the assembler from four Polished Calcite, four ender pearls and four nether wart, as it looks: Polished Calcite, the companion mod's block that the assembler makes from four calcite, for its pale, bordered body, since end stone is had only in the End, and pearls for its green. It takes no eye of ender, since a placed frame is empty and takes its eye by hand as in vanilla, so a portal costs the twelve eyes it always has. Nether wart grows only in the Nether's fortresses, so the way into the End goes through the Nether. The Shop sells calcite, and a Cobblegen Galore generator with a calcite block as its modifier makes the rest.
- The pack names the structures Skyblock Builder generates rather than leaving it to its defaults: fortresses in the Nether, End cities in the End, none in the Overworld, so no stronghold, and with it no End Portal Frame, ever comes back.
- A portal the game makes over the lava sea, where it finds no ground, stands on an obsidian floor the companion mod lays two blocks past its frame, so a team arrives with room to bridge out to a fortress; the same goes for a portal made over the Overworld's void on the way home.
- The End's floor shuts out Voidworks' extra step of Void Pressure over open void, which looks only below a position, so the pack raises the End's base pressure by one instead: End motes stay as strong as Voidworks makes them at their best.
- Bastions stay out of the Nether, so crying obsidian and netherite are still had only through Voidworks' steep Rungs; the fortresses' blaze rods and the End cities' shulker shells come free, once a team is in the last chapter.
- Until the last chapter, blazes and wither skeletons come only from the Assault and Theurgy, as before.

# The Nether and the End open with the last chapter

The Nether and the End stay, void around their islands, with the End's main island and its dragon, but a team reaches them only once it has won all four challenges. They are the last chapter's places: Quintessence is made with Voidworks, whose displacers work only in the Nether or only in the End, and whose draconic siphon takes the dragon's breath. An obsidian frame forms a Nether portal only on the island of a team that has won all four, and never off a team's island in the Overworld; in the Nether a frame always forms, so the way home is there. The End is reached by an End portal the team builds from crafted End Portal Frames, whose recipe a team unlocks with its fourth win.

We could have removed both, since nothing before the last chapter needs them and the Assault, Theurgy and the Shop give their drops, but that would have cost Voidworks its displacers and the dragon its breath. We could have left them free from the start, as void a team builds in, but a team could then build Quintessence's chain ahead of its four wins, which ADR 0007 rules out for every chain.

## Consequences

- The lock is on the portal forming, by the island's team, not on travel: a player whose team has not won all four may go through a finished team's portal. It never closes again, since a won challenge stays won.
- A void world has no stronghold and End Portal Frames can't be had in survival, so the crafted frame is the only way into the End. What its recipe takes is decided with Quintessence's chain.
- Until the last chapter, blazes and wither skeletons come only from the Assault, Theurgy and the Shop, as before.

# Banked FE flows under the energy handicap

The energy handicap cuts only a team's generators (ADR 0005). FE the team banked before the challenge, in batteries, storage, gear or an FE drive's own store, is not set aside: it flows and is spent as usual until it runs out. Banking FE ahead is one of the strategies the energy challenge rewards, and it keeps the four challenges non-linear: a team that prepares for energy plays it differently from one that walks in cold. Money, by contrast, is still set aside under the money handicap.

## Considered Options

- Setting banked FE aside, as #215 planned: storage on a playing team's island gives no FE and keeps its charge until the challenge ends. Rejected; it removes preparation as a strategy and needs a storage tag and a capability face for every battery and item.

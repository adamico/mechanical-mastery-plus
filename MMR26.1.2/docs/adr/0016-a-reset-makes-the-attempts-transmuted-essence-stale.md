# A reset makes the attempt's Transmuted Essence stale

Each Transmuted Essence a challenge's chain makes is marked with the attempt it was made in, and a Port takes only the current attempt's: a reset, or a stop and a new start, makes every one made before it stale. Before this, a reset lost only what the Port had already taken, so a team that held its Transmuted Essence back and filled the Port at once lost nothing to a reset, and a protection team gained from one, getting wave 1 back. Overproduction's cap threatened nothing; now a reset costs the attempt's output, which is time, in every challenge alike.

## Considered options

- **A free reset, told honestly as a breather.** Overproduction would then be pressure with no stakes, the "time counter in disguise" a playtester saw.
- **Each reset raises the quota.** It can't be dodged, but it piles up on a team already struggling, and grinds.
- **C.O.D. keeps its pace through a reset.** A death spiral, after which a stop is all that is left.

## Consequences

- The pressure stays driven by time since the attempt began; only the stakes changed.
- A won chain's Transmuted Essence, made with no attempt running, is never stale; the Port it would go to is shut for good anyway.
- Stale matters to a Port alone: the Shop still buys a stale one, and a Quartessence still takes it, so a reset leaves some salvage in every challenge.

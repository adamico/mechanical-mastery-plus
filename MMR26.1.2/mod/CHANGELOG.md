# Changelog

Written for the pack and its players: what Mechanical Mastery Reborn adds, or changes. A published version never changes: a fix is the next patch (`docs/agents/releases.md`).

## Unreleased

- Inflation: from the moment a team chooses a handicap, the Advanced essence its awake C.O.D. makes counts against it, and each Advanced essence the team decomposes, by hand or on a Recycler alike, counts back; the excess is the one less the other, never below zero. Voiding or storing essence doesn't lower it, and a dormant C.O.D.'s never counts. Ending the challenge forgets it. `/cod status` and Jade on C.O.D. show made, decomposed and the excess while the team plays a challenge, and it is saved with the world (#204).
- A challenge starts with a fanfare: each of the team's players online hears vanilla's challenge-complete sound wherever they stand and sees the challenge's name as a gold title, such as "Energy Challenge", with its cost below; the chat line stays. A player offline when it starts gets none of it on joining (#203).
- The energy handicap: while a team plays the energy challenge, every generator on its island gives it no energy, and goes on for every other team. A generator is a block in the `mechanicalmasteryreborn:generators` block tag, which the mod ships empty for the pack to fill; it fills its own buffer and idles, and gives its energy again when the challenge ends. Only a generator that is drawn from is stopped, not one that pushes its energy out. With Jade, a stopped generator says why, and still shows the energy its buffer holds. The lab now words the energy handicap's cost as "Your generators give no energy" (#202).
- The lab, where a team chooses a handicap to start its challenge. Its screen shows each handicap and what it costs before choosing; only energy can be chosen yet, and protection, life and money are shown as coming later. Only the team whose Skyblock Builder island the lab stands on can choose there, one challenge at a time. Each of the team's players online reads in chat which challenge it now plays. The handicap is saved with the team's state, and `/cod status` says which challenge the island's team plays; `/cod dormant` leaves it as it is. For operators, `/cod challenge start <handicap>` starts the challenge of the island they stand on, as its lab would, and `/cod challenge end` ends it, its handicap with it. Unbreakable, as C.O.D. is, and placed beside C.O.D. on every new island (#201).
- Recycling: the Recycler takes any item that isn't an essence and, in eight turns, breaks one down into what made it, by the recipe that makes it run backwards: smelting first, then the assembling types the server config names (the pack names Craftworks'), then crafting. An uncompressing recipe (an ingot from a block) is never run backwards, an ore loses to any other ingredient, a tag returns its first item, and one of what a recipe makes several of returns its share. An item no recipe makes recycles into itself, so surplus can be thrown away. Damage and enchantments are ignored; an item holding other items, such as a filled shulker box, is refused. Recycling is never recorded and never counts against Inflation (#184).
- The `mechanicalmasteryreborn:recycling` recipe type, which overrides what an item recycles into, with a KubeJS schema (#184).
- Yield: a decompose or a recycle returns a share of the full amounts, each part of an item left over as the chance of one more. A tier-1 core's, and decomposing by hand's, is a quarter, set in the server config's `recycler.yield.tier_1` (#184).
- An empty-hand right-click on a Recycler's core takes back the whole stack it holds, giving up the turns toward the work under way, so a wrong item no longer jams it. Automation still can't take it out (#191).
- C.O.D. wakes on its team's first decompose on a Recycler; decomposing by hand never wakes it. Awake, it makes Advanced essence without stopping, one every 2 seconds (the server config's `cod.waking_interval`, in ticks), about one hand drive's throughput, and pushes it into any inventory, belt or pipe beside it. Once its slot is full and nothing takes from it, the essence spills out of its top onto a pile of up to a stack; past that it is still counted as made but never spawns. When it wakes, each of the team's players online hears a beacon switch on and reads what C.O.D. now does in chat. Each team's state, whether its C.O.D. is awake, is shared by its Skyblock Builder team and saved with the world (#167).
- `/cod status` and `/cod dormant`, for operators: which team a player is in and which team's island they stand on, with whether its C.O.D. is awake; and putting that island's C.O.D. back to dormant (#167).
- A C.O.D. or a Recycler now belongs to the team whose island it stands on in Skyblock Builder's single-world mode, as the pack plays; before, every island fell back to one team for the whole world (#167).
- C.O.D., dormant, makes Advanced essence at a pace instead of refilling its slot the moment it is emptied: one every 6 seconds, a little faster than decomposing by hand, while its slot holds less than a stack. A newly placed C.O.D. still holds a full stack. The pace is the server config's `cod.dormant_interval`, in ticks. Jade shows a bar toward the next essence, with the seconds left, or that the slot is full (#196).
- An essence left on the ground fizzles when it expires, with a puff of smoke and a soft hiss, instead of vanishing silently, so the pile an awake C.O.D. spills looks like waste. It is only for show: the essence is gone as before and nothing takes its place (#199).
- EMI shows every recycle by a recipe in a Recycling tab, and each decompose's and recycle's results at the tier-1 yield, with their chance. Jade says whether a Recycler is decomposing or recycling (#184).

- Decomposing by hand: a right-click with an essence decomposes one into its mix, straight into the inventory, then rests it for 8 seconds (#166).
- The Recycler, a multiblock built block by block with no screen and no power: a tier-1 core in the middle of a 3x3's front edge, facing out so it is in reach, and any mix of hand drives, inputs, outputs and casings in the other eight places, the centre among them, with at least one drive, input and output. Recyclers may stand side by side, but not share a place (#194). Each right-click on a hand drive turns it, at most once every 5 ticks, and drives add up; eight turns decompose one essence. Inputs take essence by hand, from a hopper above or from anything beside them. Each output shows three results on a shelf, taken by a right-click or by a hopper below, filled clockwise around the centre from the front-left place, then the centre (#166, #194).
- Turning a hand drive is heard and seen: a click that rises in pitch as the decompose goes, with sparks; the crafter's sound and a puff when it decomposes; a dull click and smoke when the Recycler can't decompose, so the turn is lost (#166).
- The Recycler's parts are full blocks in white space plating, from Malcolm Riley's unused textures (CC BY 4.0). On top and bottom, the drives' rails turn to the Recycler's outer edges, so the whole build is framed. An input's front and an output's niche face the player who places them (#166).
- The `mechanicalmasteryreborn:decomposing` recipe type, which the pack writes, with a KubeJS schema; EMI shows its recipes, and Jade shows a Recycler's turns, what it holds and why it is stopped, on its core and on each of its drives (#166).
- Each decompose is recorded for the team, by tier and by way: on a Recycler, for the team whose Skyblock Builder island it stands on, or by hand (#166).
- C.O.D., dormant: its one slot holds a stack of Advanced Mechanical Essence and refills the moment it is emptied, by hand or by automation. It makes nothing else (#164).
- The essences: Basic, Regular, Improved and Advanced Mechanical Essence, which are in the Works tab, and Galvanic, Warding, Vital and Aureate Essence and Quintessence, registered under their ids but not obtainable yet (#164).

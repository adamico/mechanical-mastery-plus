# The Athanor

<BlockImage id="athanor" scale="4" />

The Athanor turns **Nullessence**, and nothing else, into **Quintessence**, with no Voidworks: the second way to
answer C.O.D.'s [last stand](cod.md). It can be crafted at any time, but does no work until your team's last stand
starts. One works to a team: a second one your team places does nothing while the first stands.

Put Nullessence in by hand or from a hopper or a pipe on any side, and take Quintessence out the same way, or from
its slot. Right-click it with an empty hand to open it.

## Spells

The Athanor works only while your team bears a handicap there. Take one on at the Athanor and your team bears it for
a fixed **spell**, harder than in its challenge from the start, and the spell can't be ended early. Spells overlap,
and one can be taken on again once it ends.

Each handicap feeds a **supply line** with what it takes from your team, as it takes it, so nothing saved up counts:

- **Energy**: your generators give their output to the Athanor and to nothing else, and no FE moves between blocks
  on your island, so what your batteries banked can't be spent. The line is fed while your generators give the
  Athanor what it asks for.
- **Life**: the hungriest [Diet](diet.md). Give a heart at the Athanor's menu, as a self-sacrifice: it becomes Essentia
  Vitae in the Athanor, more for each Rune of Self Sacrifice near it. Essentia Vitae can't be piped in.
- **Protection**: an Assault at its hardest. Its mobs drop **Null Shards** in place of Rift Shards, each stamped with
  the spell it fell in: the Athanor takes only the running spell's. While no one of your team is home the Assault
  waits, and its line counts for nothing while the spell runs on.
- **Money**: your savings are set aside, sales pay less and the Shop's prices rise, and the Athanor drains a steady
  amount from what your team earns. The line is fed while the drain can be paid.

The more lines are fed, the faster the Athanor works and the less Nullessence each Quintessence takes. Only with all
four fed does a Quintessence take less Nullessence than it cancels.

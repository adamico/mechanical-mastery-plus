# Your island and team

Every team starts on its own island in a void world: a tree, one water source, one lava source, a dormant
[C.O.D.](cod.md) in the middle with its four [Ports](cod.md) at its corners, and the [lab](lab.md) beside it.
You spawn on the grass facing C.O.D. Nothing else is out there. What the island doesn't give, an essence
[decomposes](decomposing.md) into or the Shop sells.

The Galvanic Port stands to the north-west of C.O.D., the Warding to the north-east, the Vital to the south-west
and the Aureate to the south-east. A Port is dark until its challenge starts, wears its essence's colour while it
is played, and glows in it once won. The island shows how far your team has got.

## Your team

A player who joins with no team gets one of their own, named after them, with its own island. Your team is the
players of your island. Everything on it counts for the team: C.O.D. wakes for it, challenges are played and won
by it, and the Shop's account, money and claims are its. A player who joins or leaves an island changes team with
it, and takes its chain, account and claims.

## The Manual

Every player gets this Manual once, on their first join. A lost one isn't replaced. Hold
<KeyBind id="key.guideme.guide" /> over an item, or while looking at the lab, C.O.D. or a Port, to open its page
without it, or open the whole Manual with this command:

```
/guidemec open mechanicalmasteryreborn:manual
```

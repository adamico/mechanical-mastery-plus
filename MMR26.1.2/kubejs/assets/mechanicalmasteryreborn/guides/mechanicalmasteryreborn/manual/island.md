# Your island and team

Every team starts on an island of its own in a void world: a lone island with a tree, one water source and one
lava source, a dormant [C.O.D.](cod.md) in its middle with its four [Ports](cod.md) at its corners, and the
[lab](lab.md) beside it. You spawn on the grass before C.O.D., facing it. Nothing else is out there: what the
island doesn't give, an essence [decomposes](decomposing.md) into or the Shop sells.

Each Port stands at one corner of C.O.D.: the Galvanic Port to the north-west, the Warding to the north-east,
the Vital to the south-west and the Aureate to the south-east. A Port is dark until its challenge starts, wears
its essence's colour while it is played, and glows in it once it is won, so the island shows how far your team
has got.

## Your team

A player who joins with no team gets a team of their own, named after them, with its own island. Your team is
the players of your island, and everything on this island counts for it: C.O.D. wakes for it, challenges are
chosen, played and won by it, the Shop's account and money are its, and so are its claims. A player who joins
or leaves an island takes their place in its team with them, and with it its chain, account and claims.

## The Manual

Every player is given this Manual once, on their first join. A lost one isn't replaced: hold
<KeyBind id="key.guideme.guide" /> over an item, or while looking at the lab, C.O.D. or a Port, to open its page
without it, or open the whole Manual with the command below.

```
/guidemec open mechanicalmasteryreborn:manual
```

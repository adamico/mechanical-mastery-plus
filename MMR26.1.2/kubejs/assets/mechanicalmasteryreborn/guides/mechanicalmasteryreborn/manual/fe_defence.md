# Electric Defence

<BlockImage id="fe_defence" scale="4" />

An Electric Defence guards your island on its own. Feed it FE from any side, with a cable, a battery or a
generator beside it. It stores a few shots' worth. Whenever it holds a shot's worth, it shoots the nearest hostile
mob it can see in reach, and the shot spends FE. A creeper in reach is always shot first, even with other mobs
nearer, since its blast tears up your island. Its shots are electric, not magic, so a witch takes them in full.

It never shoots players or passive mobs, and spends nothing while no hostile mob is in reach. Without FE it does
nothing, and Jade says so.

Defences are free from the start: no challenge locks them. Build and power them before your team takes on the
**protection** challenge, whose Assault sends waves of mobs over the bridges to your island.

# The last chapter

Once your team has won all four [challenges](challenges/index.md), it plays the last chapter, for good. The
Nether and the End open to it, it starts C.O.D.'s **last stand** at the lab, and it makes **Quintessence** to shut
C.O.D. down. The last chapter has no handicap and can't be lost.

## The Nether and the End

The fourth win opens the Nether: from then on, an obsidian frame lit on your island forms a portal. Lit anywhere
else in the Overworld, or before the fourth win, it won't, and says so. In the Nether a frame always forms, so the
way home is there. Only the forming is locked: anyone may walk through a portal that has formed.

The Nether is a sea of lava with floating fortresses, and no bastions. A portal the game makes over the lava, or
over the Overworld's void on the way home, stands on an obsidian floor a little wider than its frame, so you
arrive with room to bridge out.

The End is reached by an End portal you build yourself. There is no stronghold. **End Portal Frames** are assembled
once your team has won all four, and they take nether wart, which grows only in the fortresses: the way into the
End goes through the Nether. A placed frame is empty and takes its eye of ender by hand, as it always has. The End
is a plain of end stone with its main island, the dragon and its towers, End cities and chorus, and the void only
above it. The Shop sells chorus flowers.

## The last stand

With all four won, C.O.D. makes nothing until a member starts the last stand at the [lab](lab.md), whose last row
offers it. From then on C.O.D. makes **Nullessence**, and the lab awakens as the [Hermetic Lab](hermetic_lab.md).
The Lead, the margin, how C.O.D. paces itself on your team, and the win are told on [C.O.D.'s page](cod.md).

Nullessence decomposes into a mix drawn from every tier's, so each of the four chains can run on it again. Every
one C.O.D. makes counts against your Lead, whatever becomes of it. Only Quintessence put into C.O.D. moves the
Lead the other way.

## Quintessence

Quintessence is made two ways. Both unlock only once your last stand has started, and EMI marks their recipes until
then.

**The Voidworks way**: run the four chains on what Nullessence decomposes into, and assemble one of each
Transmuted Essence into a **Quartessence**. A Voidworks **Mutation Chamber** climbs Quartessence into
Quintessence by a steep Rung: each try takes several Quartessence and many motes, and may fail, which spends the
motes and keeps the Quartessence. The motes come from Voidworks' displacers, which work only in the Nether or only
in the End. A Void Crucible won't take Quartessence. A Mutation Chamber holds it and spends nothing until your last
stand has started, and Jade on it says so.

**The Hermetic Lab's way**: your awakened lab turns Nullessence into Quintessence with no Voidworks, while your
team bears the four handicaps again as **spells**, harder than in their challenges. See
[the Hermetic Lab](hermetic_lab.md).

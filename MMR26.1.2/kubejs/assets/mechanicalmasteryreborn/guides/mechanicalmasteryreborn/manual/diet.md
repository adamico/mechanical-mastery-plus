# The Diet

While your team plays the life challenge, hunger drains faster, and each player keeps a **Diet**: the foods they
have eaten lately. A food already in your Diet feeds you less each time you eat it again. A food you haven't had
for a while feeds you fully again. Living on one crop won't keep up; eat a varied menu.

A food's tooltip shows how much it feeds you now.

The Diet survives your death. It is forgotten when the life challenge ends or resets, and every food feeds fully
again. In the last chapter, the [Hermetic Lab](hermetic_lab.md)'s life spell keeps a Diet of its own, the
hungriest, from the spell's start to its end.

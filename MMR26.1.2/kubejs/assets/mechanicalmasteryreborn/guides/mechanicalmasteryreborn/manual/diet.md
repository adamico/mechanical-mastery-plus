# The Diet

While your team plays the life challenge, hunger drains faster, and each player keeps a **Diet**: the foods they
have eaten lately.

A food already in your Diet feeds you less each time you eat it again, so living on one crop won't keep up. Eat
a varied menu, and a food you haven't had for a while feeds you fully again.

A food's tooltip shows how much it feeds you now, as "Feeds N% now".

The Diet is kept only while the life challenge is played: when it ends, your Diet is forgotten and every food
feeds fully again.

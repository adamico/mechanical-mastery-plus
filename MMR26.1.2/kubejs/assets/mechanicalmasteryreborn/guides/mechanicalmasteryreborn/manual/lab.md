# The lab

<BlockImage id="lab" scale="4" />

Every island starts with a lab, and it can't be broken or made.

Right-click it to choose a **handicap**, which starts its **challenge** for your whole team. A handicap costs
your team only while its challenge is played.

For now the lab offers one: **Energy**, under which your generators give no energy. The others show as coming
later.

Your team plays one challenge at a time. Only the team whose island the lab stands on can choose at it.

# The lab

<BlockImage id="lab" scale="4" />

Every island starts with a lab, and it can't be broken or made. Right-click it to open it. Only the team whose
island it stands on can act there; anyone else sees its rows, and can do nothing with them.

Its rows, as your team gets to them:

- **A handicap**, one row for each of the four [challenges](challenges/index.md): protection, energy, life and
  money. Choose one and its challenge starts for your whole team; a handicap costs your team only while its
  challenge is played. Your team plays one challenge at a time, and a won one shows as won and can't be chosen
  again.
- **Stop**, offered only once the attempt has reset at least once: ask to stop the challenge, or take the ask
  back. It stops once every member online has asked, and the row says who has.
- **Refund**, whenever an [Assault](challenges/protection.md) has broken something on your island: how many items
  wait, and a button to open them. Take them a stack at a time, or everything that fits at once.
- **Last stand**, once your team has won all four: start C.O.D.'s [last stand](cod.md), the pack's ending. From
  then on the lab awakens as the [Hermetic Lab](hermetic_lab.md), and its refund stays a click away there.

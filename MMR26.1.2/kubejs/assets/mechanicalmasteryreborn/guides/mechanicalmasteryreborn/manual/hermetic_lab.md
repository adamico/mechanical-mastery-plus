# The Hermetic Lab

<BlockImage id="lab" p:hermetic="true" scale="4" />

Once your team starts C.O.D.'s [last stand](cod.md), your [lab](lab.md) awakens as the **Hermetic Lab**: it stands
changed, and opens a menu of its own. It turns **Nullessence** into **Quintessence**, with no Voidworks: the
second way to answer the last stand, beside the Voidworks way told on the [last chapter](last_chapter.md)'s page.

Put Nullessence in by hand or from a hopper or a pipe on any side, while the last stand runs, and take
Quintessence out the same way, or from its slot. Null Shards go in the same ways. Right-click it with an empty
hand to open it. Your team's refund is still a click away there, and once C.O.D. has shut down the menu says there
is nothing left to do.

## Spells

The Hermetic Lab works only while your team bears a handicap there. Any member takes one on at its menu, and
your team bears it for a fixed **spell**, harder than in its challenge from the start, which can't be ended
early: everyone online sees the spell's name and hears the raid horn, and the row counts down what is left. Spells
overlap, and one can be taken on again once it ends. When the pack is won, every spell ends.

Each handicap feeds a **supply line** with what it takes from your team, as it takes it, so nothing saved up
counts, and what a line still holds when its spell ends is lost. The row says whether its line is fed:

- **Energy**: your generators give their output to the Hermetic Lab and to nothing else, and no FE moves
  between blocks on your island at all, so what your batteries banked can't be spent, and FE drives and Electric
  Defences stand still. The line is fed while generators within the Hermetic Lab's reach, all together, give it
  what it asks for.
- **Life**: the hungriest [Diet](diet.md). Give a heart at the Hermetic Lab's menu, as a self-sacrifice: it becomes
  Essentia Vitae in the Hermetic Lab, more for each Rune of Self Sacrifice beside it. Only a member can give, never
  their last heart, and the Hermetic Lab holds only so much. Essentia Vitae can't be piped in.
- **Protection**: an Assault starting at its hardest waves, with explosions on and the refund as usual. Its mobs
  drop no Rift Shards: they drop **Null Shards** instead, which belong to the spell they dropped in. Put them into
  the Hermetic Lab while that spell runs, and they feed its protection line; shards left from an earlier spell are
  worthless, and the Hermetic Lab voids them. While nobody of your team is home the Assault waits, and the line
  feeds nothing, though the spell's time runs on.
- **Money**: your savings are set aside, sales pay less than under the challenge, the Shop's prices rise, and the
  Hermetic Lab drains a steady amount from what your team earns meanwhile. The line is fed while the drain can be
  paid, and what was set aside comes back when the spell ends.

The more lines are fed, the faster the Hermetic Lab works and the less Nullessence each Quintessence takes; the
menu says how many lines count, and what a Quintessence takes now. Only with all four fed does a Quintessence
take less Nullessence than it cancels. It stops while it holds less Nullessence than the next Quintessence owes,
or while its Quintessence slot is full.

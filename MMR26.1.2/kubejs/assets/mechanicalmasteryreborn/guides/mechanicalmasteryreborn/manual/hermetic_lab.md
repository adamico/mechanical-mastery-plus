# The Hermetic Lab

<BlockImage id="lab" p:hermetic="true" scale="4" />

Once your team starts C.O.D.'s [last stand](cod.md), your [lab](lab.md) awakens as the **Hermetic Lab**. It
stands changed and opens a menu of its own. It turns **Nullessence** into **Quintessence** with no Voidworks: the
second way to answer the last stand, beside the Voidworks way told on the [last chapter](last_chapter.md)'s page.

Right-click it with an empty hand to open it. Put Nullessence and Null Shards in by hand or from a hopper or a
pipe on any side, and take Quintessence out the same way, or from its slot. Your team's refund is still a click
away there. Once C.O.D. has shut down, the menu says there is nothing left to do.

## Spells

The Hermetic Lab works only while your team bears a handicap there. Any member takes one on at its menu, and the
team bears it for a fixed **spell**: harder than in its challenge from the start, and never ended early. Everyone
online sees the spell's name and hears the raid horn, and the row counts down what is left. Spells overlap, and
a spell can be taken on again once it ends. Every spell ends when the pack is won.

Each handicap feeds a **supply line** with what it takes from your team, as it takes it. Nothing saved up counts,
and what a line still holds when its spell ends is lost. The row says whether its line is fed:

- **Energy**: your generators give their output to the Hermetic Lab and to nothing else, and no FE moves between
  blocks on your island. Banked FE can't be spent, and FE drives and Electric Defences stand still. The line is fed
  while the generators within the Hermetic Lab's reach, together, give it what it asks for.
- **Life**: the hungriest [Diet](diet.md). Only hearts feed the line: give one at the Hermetic Lab's menu, as a
  self-sacrifice, and it becomes Essentia Vitae there, more for each Rune of Self Sacrifice beside it. Only a
  member can give, never their last heart, and the Hermetic Lab holds only so much. Essentia Vitae from anywhere
  else never counts.
- **Protection**: an Assault from its hardest waves, with explosions on and the refund as usual. Its mobs drop
  **Null Shards** instead of Rift Shards, and a Null Shard belongs to the spell it dropped in. Put them into the
  Hermetic Lab while that spell runs. Shards from an earlier spell are worthless, and the Hermetic Lab voids them.
  While nobody of your team is home, the Assault waits and the line feeds nothing, though the spell's time runs on.
- **Money**: your savings are set aside, sales pay less than under the challenge, the Shop's prices rise, and the
  Hermetic Lab drains a steady amount from what your team earns meanwhile. The line is fed while the drain can be
  paid. What was set aside comes back when the spell ends.

The more lines are fed, the faster the Hermetic Lab works and the less Nullessence each Quintessence takes. The
menu says how many lines count and what a Quintessence takes now. Only with all four fed does a Quintessence take
less Nullessence than it cancels. The Hermetic Lab stops while it holds less Nullessence than the next
Quintessence takes, or while its Quintessence slot is full.

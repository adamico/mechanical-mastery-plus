# The Hermetic Lab

<BlockImage id="lab" p:hermetic="true" scale="4" />

Once your team starts C.O.D.'s [last stand](cod.md), your [lab](lab.md) awakens as the **Hermetic Lab**: it stands
changed, and opens a menu of its own. It turns **Nullessence**, and nothing else, into **Quintessence**, with no
Voidworks: the second way to answer the last stand.

Put Nullessence in by hand or from a hopper or a pipe on any side, and take Quintessence out the same way, or from
its slot. Right-click it with an empty hand to open it. Your team's refund is still a click away there.

## Spells

The Hermetic Lab works only while your team bears a handicap there. Take one on at the Hermetic Lab and your team bears it for
a fixed **spell**, harder than in its challenge from the start, and the spell can't be ended early. Spells overlap,
and one can be taken on again once it ends.

Each handicap feeds a **supply line** with what it takes from your team, as it takes it, so nothing saved up counts:

- **Energy**: your generators give their output to the Hermetic Lab and to nothing else, and no FE moves between blocks
  on your island, so what your batteries banked can't be spent. The line is fed while your generators give the
  Hermetic Lab what it asks for.
- **Life**: the hungriest [Diet](diet.md). Give a heart at the Hermetic Lab's menu, as a self-sacrifice: it becomes Essentia
  Vitae in the Hermetic Lab, more for each Rune of Self Sacrifice near it. Essentia Vitae can't be piped in.
- **Protection**: an Assault at its hardest. Its mobs drop **Null Shards** in place of Rift Shards, each stamped with
  the spell it fell in: the Hermetic Lab takes only the running spell's. While no one of your team is home the Assault
  waits, and its line counts for nothing while the spell runs on.
- **Money**: your savings are set aside, sales pay less and the Shop's prices rise, and the Hermetic Lab drains a steady
  amount from what your team earns. The line is fed while the drain can be paid.

The more lines are fed, the faster the Hermetic Lab works and the less Nullessence each Quintessence takes. Only with all
four fed does a Quintessence take less Nullessence than it cancels.

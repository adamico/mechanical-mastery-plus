# Hand drive

<BlockImage id="hand_drive" scale="4" />

A **drive** turns a [Recycler](index.md), and a hand drive is turned by you. Each right-click turns it once, and
holding the click keeps it turning. It turns no faster than a player can: a click while it still turns waits for
the next turn.

Each turn is heard and seen. A click whose pitch rises as the decompose goes on, and sparks, mean the turn
counted. A dull click and smoke mean the Recycler can't run, and the turn is lost: Jade on the [core](core.md)
says why.

Turning a drive is work, as sprinting is: each turn that counts makes you hungrier, saturation first. A lost turn
costs nothing. Too hungry, you can't turn it, and it refuses as a lost turn would: eat, and it turns again.
Decomposing by hand costs no food, so you can always get going.

A Recycler's drives add up: two players on two drives run it twice as fast. A drive stands in any of the
Recycler's eight places. At a corner, its rails turn to run along the Recycler's outer edges.

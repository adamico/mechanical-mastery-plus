# Core

<BlockImage id="recycler_core_1" scale="4" p:facing="north" />

The heart of a [Recycler](index.md). It stands in the middle of one edge of the 3x3, its front facing out. It
faces you as you place it, so place it from where you want its front: the rest of the 3x3 lies behind it.

It holds the essence, or the item to recycle, that its [inputs](input.md) take in, and the work under way. Its
tier sets how many turns of its drives a decompose or a recycle takes, and how many [floors](index.md#floors)
the Recycler may have: one for each tier. Every core returns the same yield.

Right-click it with an empty hand to take back the whole stack it holds. That gives up the work under way.
Nothing else can take it out.

There are four tiers. Each higher core is made from the one below, takes fewer turns, and can be crafted only
once your team has won as many challenges as the tier it replaces:

- [Tier 2](core_2.md)
- [Tier 3](core_3.md)
- [Tier 4](core_4.md)

To upgrade, break the core and place the new one where it stood, facing the same way. Breaking it drops what it
held and gives up the work under way. The core's tooltip shows how many turns it takes.

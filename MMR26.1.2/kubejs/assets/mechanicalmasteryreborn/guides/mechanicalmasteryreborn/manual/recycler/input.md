# Input

<BlockImage id="recycler_input" scale="4" />

Where essence, or an item to recycle, goes into a [Recycler](index.md). Right-click it with what you hold, let a
hopper above drop into it, or push into it from any side with a pipe or a belt. Everything goes into the
[core](core.md), which holds one kind at a time. Nothing comes back out of an input.

What the Recycler can't take, it refuses, and a right-click tells you why: something refined, something to
uncraft instead, a core already holding another item or a full stack, or an input that isn't part of a Recycler.
A right-click never places a block against an input; sneak and right-click to place one.

It faces you as you place it, but takes in from every side.

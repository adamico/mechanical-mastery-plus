# FE drive

<BlockImage id="fe_drive" scale="4" />

An FE drive turns a [Recycler](index.md) on power, so nobody has to crank a [hand drive](hand_drive.md). Feed it
FE from any side, with a cable or a generator beside it. It stores a few turns' worth. While its Recycler has work
and the store holds a turn's worth, it turns as fast as one player cranking without a pause, and each turn spends
FE. It spends nothing while the Recycler can't run.

Its turns are heard and seen as a hand drive's are. Jade, on the drive or the [core](core.md), says why the
Recycler is stopped.

FE drives add up with each other and with hand drives: two FE drives run a Recycler twice as fast.

The **energy** challenge stops your generators, not your batteries and not this drive. It runs on what it stores
and what your batteries still give it, until that is spent.

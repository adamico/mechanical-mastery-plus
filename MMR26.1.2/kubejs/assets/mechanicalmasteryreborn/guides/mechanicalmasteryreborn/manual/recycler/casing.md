# Casing

<BlockImage id="recycler_casing" scale="4" />

A casing fills a place in a [Recycler](index.md)'s 3x3 that needs no more [drives](hand_drive.md),
[inputs](input.md) or [outputs](output.md), and does nothing else. It is the cheapest part.

# The life challenge

Its handicap is a hungrier **Diet**, and its Transmuted Essence is the **Vital Essence**, which a **Pelican**
makes from meals at the end of life's chain.

## The handicap

While your team plays life, every member grows hungry faster: sprinting, healing, cranking a hand drive and
everything else that tires you tires you more. Each member keeps a [Diet](../diet.md), the foods they have eaten
lately, and a food already in it feeds less each time it is eaten again, in hunger and saturation alike. Neo
Vitae's Essentia Vitae comes first from your own health, and health comes back only from food, so a team living
on one crop bleeds slowly. Only a varied farm keeps up.

The handicap goes on in Neo Vitae's Demon Realm, so plan what you eat for the trip. Running empty starves as it
always does, and nothing worse.

## The chain

Starting the challenge sets life's chain machines to work on your island. The chain ends at the Pelican.

## The Pelican

<BlockImage id="pelican" scale="4" p:lit="true" />

The **Pelican** is a glass vessel the Shop sells once your team starts the challenge. It makes the Vital Essence
from meals, and eats nothing else: not raw food, not ingredients, and never rotten flesh. It takes them only from
automation, on any side, and eats each as it comes in.

What it has eaten since its last Vital Essence is its batch. Once the batch holds enough saturation, enough kinds of
meal and a high enough average quality, all three at once, its contents light up and it digests the batch, eating
nothing meanwhile. Then it gives one Vital Essence, and eats again once the essence is taken out. A varied menu of
good meals fills it soonest; Jade shows how far its batch has got, and EMI has a page on it.

Like every chain machine, it sits idle until your team starts life. A stop or a reset loses its batch, even one it
was digesting; a win keeps it.

## The reset

A reset forgets every member's Diet, and a new one starts with the attempt.

## In the last stand

In the [last chapter](../last_chapter.md), your team can take the life handicap on again as a **spell** at the
[Hermetic Lab](../hermetic_lab.md), under the hungriest Diet of all. Only hearts given at the Hermetic Lab itself
feed its life line.

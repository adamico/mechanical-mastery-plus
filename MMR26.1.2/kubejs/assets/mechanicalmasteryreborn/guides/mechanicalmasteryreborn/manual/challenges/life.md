# The life challenge

Its handicap is a hungrier **Diet**, and its Transmuted Essence is the **Vital Essence**, infused at the end of
Neo Vitae's chain.

## The handicap

While your team plays life, every member grows hungry faster: sprinting, healing, cranking a hand drive and
everything else that tires you tires you more. Each member keeps a [Diet](../diet.md), the foods they have eaten
lately: a food already in it feeds less each time it is eaten again, in hunger and saturation alike. Neo Vitae's
Essentia Vitae comes first from your own health, and health comes back only from food, so a team living on one
crop bleeds slowly. Only a varied farm keeps up.

The handicap goes on in Neo Vitae's Demon Realm, so plan what you eat for the trip. Running empty starves as it
always does, and nothing worse.

## The chain

Starting the challenge sets Neo Vitae's **Hellfire Forge** and **Ara Vitae** to work on your island. Until then
they can be crafted, and may come out of a mix, but do nothing.

The chain climbs Neo Vitae from the blood your team gives up to a tier-3 Ara Vitae, reached through the Demon
Realm, where the **Vital Essence** is infused from a Tabula Spiritus with blood.

## The reset

A reset forgets every member's Diet, and a new one starts with the attempt.

## In the last stand

In the [last chapter](../last_chapter.md), your team can take the life handicap on again as a **spell** at the
[Hermetic Lab](../hermetic_lab.md), under the hungriest Diet of all, which starts with the spell and is
forgotten when it ends. Only hearts feed its life line: a member gives one at the Hermetic Lab itself, as a
self-sacrifice, and it becomes Essentia Vitae there. Essentia Vitae from anywhere else, a tank, an altar or a
mob, never counts.

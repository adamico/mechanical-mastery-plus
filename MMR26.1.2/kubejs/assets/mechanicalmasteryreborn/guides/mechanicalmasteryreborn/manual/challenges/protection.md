# The protection challenge

Its handicap is an **Assault** on your island, and its Transmuted Essence is the **Warding Essence**, assembled
from what the Assault's mobs drop.

## The Assault

When the challenge starts, four platforms of **Assault Stone**, which can't be broken and drops nothing, are
built in the void off each side of C.O.D., each with a bridge back to your island. A gate opens on one of them at
random and sends waves of mobs over its bridge, hunting your team.

The waves grow harder step by step: more and tougher mobs, then new kinds, from the zombies and skeletons of the
first waves to blazes, endermen, breezes and evokers deep in. Each step ends with a **breather**, a longer pause,
and the gate moves to another side at random. The bar at the top of your screen says which wave is on and from
which side. A wave your team doesn't clear in time fails, and the gate opens again at once, at the wave it had
reached. The waves stop only when your team fills its Port, Overproduction resets the challenge, or your team stops
it.

The Assault waits while no member of your team is on the island, and goes on from where it stood when one comes
back. The mobs already out stay, and Overproduction runs on. A mob knocked off a bridge dies in the void with its drop.

**Explosions** damage your island while the Assault runs, claimed or not: they are kept on for your team until
the Assault ends. Its endermen pick up no blocks and its blazes light nothing, so blasts are the one way it damages
your base. [Defences](../fe_defence.md) fight its mobs for you, and they are free from the start, so build them
ahead.

## The refund

Whatever the Assault's mobs break on your island drops nothing. It is held for your team instead: each block as
a careful mining by hand would give it, with what it held, and each item frame or painting with what hung in it.
When the Assault ends, by a win, a reset or a stop, it all waits at the [lab](../lab.md) as your **refund**.
Everyone online reads how much waits there, and so does a member who logs in later while any is left. Refunds add
up and never expire. Your own blasts, and a home gate's mobs, are not refunded.

## The reset

A reset ends the Assault, hands over its refund, and sends a new Assault from its first wave. Your team keeps its
Rift Shards.

## The chain

Starting the challenge opens the chain's recipes, all made on an Assembler:

- Only the Assault's mobs, and a home gate's, drop **Rift Shards**: the deeper waves' harder mobs give more.
  Nothing crafts them, and they recycle only into less of themselves.
- **Ward Tallow**, a fluid, is rendered from the Assault's rotten flesh and bone meal with steam. It is in no mix,
  and the Shop never buys it.
- **Obsidian Flakes** come only off a placed obsidian block a Harvester works with magma slurry, which breaks the
  block. Breaking obsidian by hand gives obsidian.
- A **Charged Rift Shard** is Rift Shards charged with tar, Obsidian Flakes and Ward Tallow.
- Charged Rift Shards, sealed with plastic, make the **Warding Essence**.

## The home gate

Once your team has won protection, the Shop sells the **home gate's pearl**, which no recipe makes. Throw it on
your own island to open a smaller gate of your own whenever you like: a few waves, which drop Rift Shards too,
fewer than the Assault, so a won chain goes on making Warding Essence. It opens nowhere else, and what its mobs
break is not refunded.

## In the last stand

In the [last chapter](../last_chapter.md), your team can take the protection handicap on again as a **spell** at
the [Hermetic Lab](../hermetic_lab.md). An Assault opens at once from its hardest waves, with explosions on and
the refund as usual. Its mobs drop **Null Shards** instead of Rift Shards, which feed the Hermetic Lab's
protection line while that spell runs.

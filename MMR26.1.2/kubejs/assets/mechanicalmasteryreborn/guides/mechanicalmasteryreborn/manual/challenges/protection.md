# The protection challenge

Its handicap is an **Assault** on your island, and its Transmuted Essence is the **Warding Essence**, assembled
from what the Assault's mobs drop.

## The Assault

When the challenge starts, four platforms of **Assault Stone**, which can't be broken and drops nothing, are
built in the void off each side of C.O.D., with a bridge from each back to your island. One gate opens on one of
them at random, and sends waves of mobs over its bridge, hunting your team.

The waves grow harder step by step: more and tougher mobs, then new kinds, from the zombies and skeletons of the
first waves to blazes, endermen, breezes and evokers deep in. At the end of each step comes a **breather**, a
longer pause before the next wave, and the gate moves to another of the four sides at random: the bar at the
top of your screen says which wave is on and from which side it comes. A wave your team doesn't clear in time
fails, and the gate opens again at once, at the wave it had reached. The waves don't stop until your team fills
its Port, Inflation resets the challenge, or your team stops it.

The Assault waits while no member of your team is on the island, and goes on from where it stood when one comes
back; the mobs already out stay, and Inflation runs on. A mob knocked off a bridge dies in the void with its
drop.

**Explosions** damage your island while the Assault runs, claimed or not: they are kept on for your team, and
turned back on should anyone turn them off, until the Assault ends. Its endermen pick up no blocks, and its
blazes light nothing, so blasts are the one way it damages your base. [Defences](../fe_defence.md) fight its
mobs for you, and they are free from the start, so build them ahead.

## The refund

Whatever the Assault's mobs break on your island drops nothing, and is held for your team instead: each block as a
careful mining by hand would give it, with what it held, and each item frame or painting with what hung in it.
When the Assault ends, by a win, a reset or a stop, it all waits at the [lab](../lab.md) as your **refund**:
everyone online reads how much waits there, and so does a member who logs in later while any is left. Open the
lab's refund row and take it a stack at a time, or everything that fits at once. Refunds add up and never
expire. Your own blasts, and a home gate's mobs, are not refunded.

## The reset

A reset ends the Assault, hands over its refund, and sends a new Assault from its first wave. Your team keeps
its Rift Shards.

## The chain

Starting the challenge opens the chain's recipes, all made on an Assembler:

- Only the Assault's mobs, and a home gate's, drop **Rift Shards**: the deeper waves' harder mobs give more.
  Nothing crafts them, and they recycle only into less of themselves.
- **Ward Tallow**, a fluid, is rendered from the Assault's rotten flesh and bone meal with steam. It is in no mix,
  and the Shop never buys it.
- **Obsidian Flakes** come only off a placed obsidian block a Harvester works with magma slurry, which breaks the
  block; breaking obsidian by hand gives obsidian.
- A **Charged Rift** is Rift Shards charged with tar, Obsidian Flakes and Ward Tallow.
- Charged Rifts, sealed with plastic, make the **Warding Essence**.

## The home gate

Once your team has won protection, the Shop sells the **home gate's pearl**, which no recipe makes. Throw it on
your own island to open a smaller gate of your own whenever you like: a short one of a few waves, which gives
Rift Shards too, fewer than the Assault, so a won chain goes on making Warding Essence. It opens nowhere else, and
what its mobs break is not refunded.

## In the last stand

In the [last chapter](../last_chapter.md), your team can take the protection handicap on again as a **spell** at the
[Hermetic Lab](../hermetic_lab.md). An Assault opens at once, starting at its hardest waves, with explosions on
and the refund as usual. Its mobs drop no Rift Shards: they drop **Null Shards** instead, which belong to the
spell they dropped in. Put them into the Hermetic Lab while that spell runs, and they feed its protection line;
once the spell ends they are worthless, and a later spell's Assault drops its own. While nobody of your team is
home the Assault waits, and the line feeds nothing, though the spell's time runs on.

# The challenges

Your team plays four challenges, one at a time and in any order, each against the tier of essence
[C.O.D.](../cod.md) makes at the time: **protection**, **energy**, **life** and **money**. Each one is a way
back up from what you decompose, through its own mods, to its own **Transmuted Essence**: Warding, Galvanic,
Vital and Aureate. Winning one blocks the tier C.O.D. makes, so it drops to the tier below, which is harder
and longer to decompose, and the next challenge is played against that.

- [Protection](protection.md): an Assault on your island, to the Warding Essence.
- [Energy](energy.md): your generators give nothing, to the Galvanic Essence.
- [Life](life.md): a hungrier Diet, to the Vital Essence.
- [Money](money.md): poorer sales and your savings set aside, to the Aureate Essence.

The rules below hold for every challenge. Each one's page tells what is its own.

## Starting one

Any member chooses a handicap at the [lab](../lab.md), and the challenge starts for the whole team: everyone
online sees its name, reads what it costs, and hears the raid horn. Only the team whose island the lab stands on
can choose there, and a won challenge can't be chosen again.

The **handicap** is what the challenge costs your team, and it costs you only while the challenge is played.

## The chain

Each challenge's **chain** is the path its mods take you from what you decompose to its Transmuted Essence. A
chain is locked until your team starts its challenge:

- Its machines, such as Theurgy's apparatus or the Ara Vitae, can be crafted at any time, and may well come out
  of a mix, but they do no work on your island until your team starts the challenge. Jade on one says which
  challenge unlocks it, and so does its tooltip.
- Its own recipes, such as its Transmuted Essence's, can't be made until then, on any machine.
- EMI marks every recipe that waits with a sign in its corner, naming the challenge, and the mark goes the
  moment your team starts it.

A chain stays unlocked past a win and past a reset. A stop locks it again, until your team starts the
challenge anew. A chain one challenge opened stays open through the challenges that follow.

## Inflation

While your team plays a challenge, C.O.D. makes its tier of essence ever faster, without a ceiling. Each one it
makes that your team doesn't decompose, by hand or on a Recycler, adds to **Inflation**: what it made minus what
you decomposed since the challenge began. Keeping or voiding essence doesn't lower it; only decomposing does, and
a stock decomposed early counts against what C.O.D. makes next.

Everyone online sees Inflation on the bar at the top of their screen, under the challenge's name, and Jade on
C.O.D. shows it too. As Inflation nears its cap, a bell rings and a warning is read in chat, once, and again
only if Inflation falls back and climbs again.

## The reset

Should Inflation reach its cap, the challenge **resets**, as though it had just begun: C.O.D. falls back to the
pace it woke at, Inflation starts from nothing, and what was put into the Port toward the quota is lost. Your
island, machines, items, chain and handicap stay as they are. What the handicap took is kept aside until the
challenge ends, and each page says what else its reset does. Everyone online hears a beacon switch off and
reads that the challenge was reset.

## The stop

Once an attempt has reset at least once, the lab offers to **stop** the challenge, to take on another or to come
back to it better prepared. Any member asks to stop there, or takes the ask back, and the challenge stops once
every member online has asked; a member who logs out drops their ask, and one who logs in counts from then on.

A stop ends the handicap and Inflation, gives back what the handicap took, and locks the chain again until your
team starts the challenge anew. Your team keeps every item. Any challenge not won, the stopped one too, can start
at once.

## The Port and the quota

Each challenge is won by filling its **Port**, at one of C.O.D.'s corners, with its **quota** of its Transmuted
Essence: right-click the Port with the essence, or feed it from a hopper or a pipe on any side. A Port takes only
its own essence, only while its challenge is played, and never more than the quota, so the rest stays where it
was. Jade on the Port shows how far your team has got, and the Port wears its essence's colour while it is
open.

The quota grows with each tier below the one you started at, faster than the better core a win unlocks speeds
you up, so each challenge takes longer than the last.

## The win

The Port that takes the last essence of the quota wins the challenge: everyone online sees it won, hears the
fanfare, and reads that C.O.D. makes the tier below from now on. The win gives back what the handicap took,
blocks the tier C.O.D. made for good, and lets your team craft the next tier of [core](../recycler/core.md). The
Port glows, won, and the chain stays open. A won chain goes on making its Transmuted Essence, and the Shop buys
every one but the Aureate.

Once all four are won, C.O.D. makes nothing until your team starts the [last chapter](../last_chapter.md).

# The challenges

Your team plays four challenges, one at a time and in any order, each against the tier of essence
[C.O.D.](../cod.md) makes at the time: **protection**, **energy**, **life** and **money**. Each climbs from
what you decompose, through its own mods, to its own **Transmuted Essence**. Winning one blocks the tier
C.O.D. makes, so it drops to the tier below, which is harder and longer to decompose. The next challenge is
played against that.

- [Protection](protection.md): an Assault on your island, to the Warding Essence.
- [Energy](energy.md): your generators give nothing, to the Galvanic Essence.
- [Life](life.md): a hungrier Diet, to the Vital Essence.
- [Money](money.md): poorer sales and your savings set aside, to the Aureate Essence.

The rules below hold for every challenge. Each challenge's page tells what is its own.

## Starting one

Any member chooses a handicap at the [lab](../lab.md), and the challenge starts for the whole team. Everyone
online sees its name, reads what it costs and hears the raid horn, and [C.O.D.](../cod.md) answers with a ritual
that anyone on your island sees and hears. The **handicap** is what the challenge costs
your team, and it costs you only while the challenge is played.

## The chain

A challenge's **chain** is the path its mods take you from what you decompose to its Transmuted Essence. It is
locked until your team starts the challenge:

- Its machines, such as Theurgy's apparatus or the Ara Vitae, can be crafted at any time and may come out of a
  mix, but do no work on your island until then. Jade on one says which challenge unlocks it, and so does its
  tooltip.
- Its own recipes, such as its Transmuted Essence's, can't be made until then, on any machine.
- EMI marks every recipe that waits with a sign in its corner, naming the challenge. The mark goes the moment
  your team starts it.

A chain stays unlocked past a win and past a reset, and through the challenges that follow. A stop locks it
again until your team starts the challenge anew.

## Overproduction

While your team plays a challenge, C.O.D. makes its tier of essence ever faster, without a ceiling. **Overproduction**
is what it has made minus what you have decomposed, by hand or on a Recycler, since the challenge began. Keeping
or voiding essence doesn't lower it. A stock decomposed early counts against what C.O.D. makes next.

Everyone online sees Overproduction on the bar at the top of their screen, under the challenge's name, and Jade on
C.O.D. shows it too. As Overproduction nears its cap, a bell rings and a warning is read in chat, once, and again only
if Overproduction falls back and climbs again.

## The reset

When Overproduction reaches its cap, the challenge **resets**, as though it had just begun: C.O.D. falls back to the
pace it woke at, Overproduction starts from nothing, and what was put into the Port is lost. So is every Transmuted
Essence the attempt made: it turns **stale**, and the Port takes it no more. Your island, machines, items, chain and
handicap stay as they are. What the handicap took is kept aside until the challenge ends. Each
challenge's page says what else its reset does. Everyone online hears a beacon switch off and reads that the
challenge was reset.

## The stop

Once an attempt has reset at least once, the lab offers to **stop** the challenge, to take on another or to come
back to it better prepared. Any member asks to stop there, or takes the ask back. The challenge stops once every
member online has asked. A member who logs out drops their ask; one who logs in counts from then on.

A stop ends the handicap and Overproduction, gives back what the handicap took, and locks the chain again until your
team starts the challenge anew. Your team keeps every item, though the Transmuted Essence the stopped attempt made
is stale once a challenge starts again. Any challenge not won, the stopped one too, can start
at once.

## The Port and the quota

A challenge is won by filling its **Port**, at one of C.O.D.'s corners, with its **quota** of its Transmuted
Essence: right-click the Port with the essence, or feed it from a hopper or a pipe on any side. A Port takes only
its own essence, only while its challenge is played, and never more than the quota. Jade on the Port shows how
far your team has got, and the Port wears its essence's colour while it is open.

Each Transmuted Essence your chain makes while you play its challenge is marked with the attempt it was made in. A
Port takes only the current attempt's, so essence held back is lost to a reset as surely as what the Port took.
A stale essence looks dimmed and its tooltip says it was made before the last reset; a Port refuses it, leaving it
in the hand, hopper or pipe that offered it, and a hand is told why. Stale and current essence don't stack together.
A stale essence isn't wasted: the Shop still buys it, and it still goes into a Quartessence. Essence from before
this rule, and a won chain's, is never stale.

The quota grows with each tier below the one you started at, faster than the better core a win unlocks speeds
you up, so each challenge takes longer than the last.

## The win

The Port that takes the last essence of the quota wins the challenge. Everyone online sees it won, hears the
fanfare and reads that C.O.D. makes the tier below from now on. The win gives back what the handicap took, blocks
the tier C.O.D. made for good, and lets your team craft the next tier of [core](../recycler/core.md). The Port
glows, and the chain stays open. A won chain goes on making its Transmuted Essence, and the Shop buys every one
but the Aureate. A won challenge can't be played again.

Once all four are won, C.O.D. makes nothing until your team starts the [last chapter](../last_chapter.md).

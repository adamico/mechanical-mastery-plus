# The energy challenge

Its handicap stops your **generators**, and its Transmuted Essence is the **Galvanic Essence**, incubated at
the end of Theurgy's chain.

## The handicap

While your team plays energy, every generator on your island gives it no energy: Wireworks' engines, turbines and
solar panels among them. A stopped generator fills its own buffer and idles, and Jade on it says why. It goes on
for every other team.

Your batteries, storage, gear and [FE drives](../recycler/fe_drive.md) are not stopped: whatever FE your team
banked ahead flows and is spent as usual, until it runs out. Banking FE ahead is part of playing the energy
challenge.

## The chain

Starting the challenge sets Theurgy's apparatus to work on your island: the Sal Ammoniac Accumulator, the
Liquefaction Cauldron, the Fermentation and Digestion Vats, the Calcination Oven, the Distiller, the Mercury
Catalyst, the Incubator and the Reformation Array's Sulfuric Flux Emitter. Until then they can be crafted, but do
nothing. Jade on each shows how far its work has got.

The chain climbs Theurgy's processing from what you decompose up to its salts, sulfurs and mercury, and ends at
the Incubator, where mercury crystal, mineral salt and redstone sulfur are incubated into the **Galvanic
Essence**.

## In the last stand

In the [last chapter](../last_chapter.md), your team can take energy on again as a **spell** at the
[Hermetic Lab](../hermetic_lab.md), harder than the challenge: your generators give their output to the
Hermetic Lab and to nothing else, and no FE moves between blocks on your island at all, so banked FE, FE drives
and Electric Defences all stand still. The line is fed while generators within the Hermetic Lab's reach give it
what it asks for.

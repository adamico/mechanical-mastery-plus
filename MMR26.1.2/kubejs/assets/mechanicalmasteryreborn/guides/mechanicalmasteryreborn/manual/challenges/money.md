# The money challenge

Its handicap makes the Shop costly, and its Transmuted Essence, the **Aureate Essence**, is bought there rather
than made.

## The handicap

When your team starts money, all the money it has is **set aside**, so the challenge is paid only with what you
earn under the handicap, and every sale to the Shop pays only a share of its price, by hand or by Seller. Buying
costs what it always does. When the challenge ends, by a win or a stop, what was set aside comes back, with
whatever you earned meanwhile. A reset sets aside what you earned so far as well.

The challenge can't start until your team has a Shop account, which comes a moment after its island does.

## The Offers

Starting money gives your team the challenge's **Permit** for good, which opens its Offers in the Shop:

- Sell Offers for the challenge's own fluids, such as Essentia Vitae, lava, liquified experience, sal ammoniac
  and superheated steam, and for a few blocks, such as hay and dried kelp. Sellers sell them for you.
- The **Essence Sell Permit**, which lets you sell the Galvanic, Warding and Vital Essences, never the Aureate.
- The **Ore Buy Permit**, which lets you buy ore blocks.
- The **Aureate Buy Permit**, which opens the Aureate Essence's Offer: the only source of it.

Permits are bought once and kept for good, even past a stop. The Oil Buy Permit, the raw ores, the seeds, the
saplings and the animals need no challenge.

## The chain

Money's chain locks nothing: it is paid for, not built. Fill the quota by selling what your factory makes and
buying the Aureate Essence with it.

## In the last stand

In the [last chapter](../last_chapter.md), your team can take money on again as a **spell** at the
[Hermetic Lab](../hermetic_lab.md), harder than the challenge: your savings are set aside, sales pay less than
under the challenge, the Shop's prices rise, and the Hermetic Lab drains a steady amount from what you earn
meanwhile to feed its money line, fed only while the drain can be paid.

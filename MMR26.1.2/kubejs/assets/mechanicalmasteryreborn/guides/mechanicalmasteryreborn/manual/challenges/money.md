# The money challenge

Its handicap makes the Shop costly, and its Transmuted Essence, the **Aureate Essence**, is bought there rather
than made.

## The handicap

When your team starts the money challenge, all its money is **set aside**: the challenge is paid only with what you
earn under the handicap. Every sale to the Shop, by hand or by Seller, pays only a share of its price. Buying
costs what it always does. When the challenge ends, by a win or a stop, what was set aside comes back, with what
you earned meanwhile. A reset sets aside what you earned so far as well.

The challenge can't start until your team has a Shop account, which comes a moment after its island does.

## The Offers

Starting the money challenge gives your team its **Permit**, which opens the challenge's Offers in the Shop. Your
team keeps it through a win or a reset, loses it when it stops the challenge, and is given it again when it starts
the challenge anew: the Offers are the money chain, locked and unlocked as any other chain is. The Permit opens:

- Sell Offers for the challenge's own fluids, such as sal ammoniac and
  superheated steam, and for a few blocks, such as hay and dried kelp. Sellers sell them for you. A fluid only the
  Shop gives, as water and lava, is never sold back.
- Sell Offers for the Galvanic, Warding and Vital Essences, never the Aureate. Each sells for more than what made
  it, so a won chain's extras are a profit; under the challenge's share of a sale they are a loss, so they help
  with the quota but never pay for it outright.
- The ore blocks.
- The **Aureate Essence**: the only source of it.

The Shop sells no Permit. Crude oil, the raw ores, the seeds, the saplings and the animals need no challenge.

## The chain

Fill the quota by selling what your factory makes and buying the Aureate Essence with it.

## In the last stand

In the [last chapter](../last_chapter.md), your team can take the money handicap on again as a **spell** at the
[Hermetic Lab](../hermetic_lab.md), harder than the challenge: your savings are set aside, sales pay less than
under the challenge, the Shop's prices rise, and the Hermetic Lab drains a steady amount from what you earn
meanwhile to feed its money line.

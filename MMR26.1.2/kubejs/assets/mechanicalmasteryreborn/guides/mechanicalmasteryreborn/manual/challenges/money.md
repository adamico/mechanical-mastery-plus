# The money challenge

Its handicap makes the Shop costly, and its Transmuted Essence, the **Aureate Essence**, is minted from coins of
copper, iron and gold.

## The handicap

When your team starts the money challenge, all its money is **set aside**: the challenge is paid only with what you
earn under the handicap. Every sale to the Shop, by hand or by Seller, pays only a share of its price. Buying
costs what it always does. When the challenge ends, by a win or a stop, what was set aside comes back, with what
you earned meanwhile. A reset sets aside what you earned so far as well.

The challenge can't start until your team has a Shop account, which comes a moment after its island does.

## The Offers

Starting the money challenge gives your team its **Permit**, which opens the challenge's Offers in the Shop. Your
team keeps it through a win or a reset, loses it when it stops the challenge, and is given it again when it starts
the challenge anew: the Offers are the money chain, locked and unlocked as any other chain is. The Permit opens:

- Sell Offers for a few blocks, such as hay and dried kelp. Sellers sell them for you. The Shop buys no fluid.
- Sell Offers for the four Transmuted Essences, the Aureate among them. Each sells for more than what made it, so a
  won chain's extras are a profit; under the challenge's share of a sale they are a loss, so they help with the
  quota but never pay for it outright. The Shop sells none of them.
- The ore blocks.

The Shop never buys a coin, so your gold is either sold or minted, never both.

The Shop sells no Permit. Crude oil, the raw ores, the seeds, the saplings and the animals need no challenge.

## The chain

The mint runs on Assemblers, and its recipes unlock when the challenge starts, as every chain's do:

- **Melt**: an ingot of copper, iron or gold with lava, into that metal's molten metal. Only the Shop sells lava, so
  every melt is paid for by what your factory sells.
- **Cast**: the molten metal poured into a **Mold**, which makes that metal's cast.
- **Cool and shakeout**: the cast cooled with water and knocked out of its Mold, giving the metal's **Coin** and
  **Burnt Sand**.
- **Wash**: Burnt Sand washed back into sand.
- **Mold**: sand and clay with water, making the next Mold.
- **Exchange**: a team short of a metal trades coins up, copper coins for an iron one and iron coins for a gold one,
  at a loss against the metal they were minted from.
- **Seal**: coins of all three metals sealed into an Aureate Essence.

An Aureate sealed during the challenge carries its attempt, so a reset makes it stale and the Port refuses it.

## In the last stand

In the [last chapter](../last_chapter.md), your team can take the money handicap on again as a **spell** at the
[Hermetic Lab](../hermetic_lab.md), harder than the challenge: your savings are set aside, sales pay less than
under the challenge, the Shop's prices rise, and the Hermetic Lab drains a steady amount from what you earn
meanwhile to feed its money line.

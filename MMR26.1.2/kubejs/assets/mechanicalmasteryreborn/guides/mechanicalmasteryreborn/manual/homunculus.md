# The Homunculus

A **Homunculus** is a small made servant that works the hand steps of [life's chain](challenges/life.md) for
you, such as the Cutting Board. It lives at its **Homunculus Stand** and works near it.

## Kindling one

Nothing crafts a Homunculus. Set a lightning rod on a clay block and use a **Vital Spark** on it: a harmless
strike calls it down, the clay and the rod become its two-block-tall stand, and the Homunculus rises beside it.
The spark is used up. Vital Sparks are made only while your team plays life or has won it, and a spark won't
take while life's chain is locked. The Homunculus needs room beside its stand.

## Waking and feeding it

A new Homunculus is full, but stands as a statue until a jumpstart of FE, from any source, reaches its stand.
Awake, it works off its full belly, then eats from the food you put in its stand, working faster on a better
meal. Once it has nothing left to eat it falls asleep, a statue again, until another jumpstart. Jade shows
whether it is awake, how long it is fed for and what its stand holds.

## Its work

Sneak-click it with an empty hand to assign it, then right-click a storage within reach of its stand to make it
its **Supply**. It takes items from its Supply one at a time and right-clicks the blocks near its stand with
them, as the player who kindled it. If a block refuses an item, it puts the item back and takes another.

Right-click a block ringed by eight hoppers instead to make it its **Table**: it sets the meals from its Supply
down there, such as feasts, pies and cakes, to serve them, and the hoppers catch what each meal drops.

It stands still while life's chain is locked, as after a stop, and works on through a reset.

## Death and its stand

A dead Homunculus leaves its Vital Spark, spent. Used on its stand, it raises a new one with an empty belly, so
a death costs food, not a new spark. Breaking the stand drops it and spills its food, and its Homunculus stays a
statue for good.

# Decomposing and recycling

## Decomposing

Every resource starts as an essence. To **decompose** one is to turn it into its **mix**: the items it holds, in
fixed amounts. EMI's Decomposing category shows every essence's mix.

A decompose returns the **yield** of the mix, not the whole of it: a share of each item, and a chance of one more
from what is left over. So a mix holds on average, not every time. The yield is the same by hand and on every
core.

**By hand**: right-click an essence that has a mix. What it gives goes into your inventory, and what doesn't fit
drops. The essence then rests a while, shown on your hotbar. This is the slow way.

**On a [Recycler](recycler/index.md)**: put essence into its [input](recycler/input.md) and turn its
[drive](recycler/hand_drive.md). It is much faster, and its [core](recycler/core.md)'s tier sets how fast. The
mix lands on its [outputs](recycler/output.md).

Your team's first decompose on a Recycler wakes [C.O.D.](cod.md). Decomposing by hand never does.

## Recycling

To **recycle** is to take an item that isn't an essence apart, on a Recycler only, for part of what made it. It
undoes one step of crafting or assembling, never smelting or cooking. It is how you get below what your essence
decomposes into, and how you throw surplus away. EMI's Recycling category shows what an item recycles into.
Recycling never wakes C.O.D.

Recycling stops at **refined**: anything smelted or cooked, such as ingots, glass, stone and food, and any material
made from one raw item alone, such as planks, sandstone, paper, bone meal or a dye. Sell refined surplus at the
Shop. A thing you wear, or a block that holds things, such as a furnace, still recycles. A block of ingots can't be
recycled: uncraft it, which loses nothing. A **raw** item, one nothing crafts, such as dirt, cobblestone, an ore or
a nugget, recycles into less of itself. That is how you throw raw surplus away.

# Decomposing and recycling

## Decomposing

Every resource starts as an essence. To **decompose** an essence is to turn it into its **mix**: the items it
holds, in fixed amounts. EMI's Decomposing category shows which essences have a mix, and what it holds.

A decompose never returns the whole mix, only its **yield** of it: a share of each item, and of whatever is left
over a chance of one more. So a mix holds on average, not every time.

**By hand**: right-click an essence that has a mix. What it gives goes straight into your inventory, and what doesn't fit drops.
The essence then rests a while, shown on your hotbar, so this is the slow way. Decomposing by hand always has the
lowest core's yield.

**On a [Recycler](recycler/index.md)**: put essence into its [input](recycler/input.md) and turn its
[drive](recycler/hand_drive.md). It is much faster, and its [core](recycler/core.md)'s tier sets its yield.
The mix lands on its [outputs](recycler/output.md).

Your team's first decompose on a Recycler wakes [C.O.D.](cod.md). Decomposing by hand never does.

## Recycling

To **recycle** is to take an item that isn't an essence apart, on a Recycler only, for part of what made it.
It goes one band down, undoing only the crafting or assembling that made the item, never smelting or other
cooking. It is how you get further down than what your essence decomposes into, and how you throw surplus away.
EMI's Recycling category shows what an item recycles into. Recycling never wakes C.O.D.

It stops at **refined**: anything smelted or cooked, such as ingots, glass, stone and food, and any material made
from one raw item alone, such as planks, sandstone, paper, bone meal or a dye, can't be recycled. A thing you wear,
or a block that holds things, such as a furnace, still recycles. Sell refined surplus at the Shop instead. A block of ingots can't be recycled
either: uncraft it instead, which loses nothing. A **raw** item, one nothing crafts, such as dirt, cobblestone, an
ore or a nugget, recycles into less of itself, so feeding it back is how you throw raw surplus away.

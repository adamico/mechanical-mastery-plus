# C.O.D.

<BlockImage id="cod" scale="4" />

C.O.D. stands on every island, and it can't be broken. It makes Advanced Mechanical Essence, and nothing else.
Right-click it to open its one slot, and take the essence it holds.

**Dormant**, as it starts, it holds a stack of essence, and makes one more at a steady pace whenever it holds less
than a stack. It puts nothing out of itself.

**Awake**: your team's first decompose on a [Recycler](recycler/index.md) wakes it, and from then on it makes
essence much faster, without stopping. It pushes each one into whatever takes items beside it, such as a hopper
or a pipe. Once its slot is full, essence piles up on its top, up to a stack, unless a block stands there. Past
that, what it makes goes nowhere: a pile on top means your Recycler is falling behind.

While your team plays a [challenge](challenges/index.md), C.O.D. makes essence ever faster. Each one it makes and
you don't decompose adds to **Inflation**: should Inflation reach its cap, your challenge starts over.

## The Ports

Four **Ports** stand at C.O.D.'s corners, one for each [challenge](challenges/index.md)'s Transmuted Essence:
Galvanic for energy, Warding for protection, Vital for life and Aureate for money. They can't be broken. A Port is
dark until your team starts its challenge, wears its essence's colour while the challenge is played, and glows in it
once it is won: light blue for the Galvanic, light grey for the Warding, lime for the Vital and yellow for the
Aureate.

A challenge is won by filling its Port with its **quota** of that essence: right-click it with the essence, or
feed it from a hopper or a pipe on any side. It takes only its own essence, only while its challenge is played,
and never more than the quota, so what's left over stays where it was. The quota grows with each tier below the
one your team started at. Jade on the Port shows how far your team has got, and a reset loses what was put in.

Winning ends the challenge, gives back what its handicap took, and blocks the tier C.O.D. made: from then on it
makes the tier below, at its pace between challenges. A won challenge can't be played again.

## The last stand

Once your team has won all four challenges, C.O.D. makes nothing until you start its **last stand** at the
[lab](lab.md), in the [last chapter](last_chapter.md). From then on it makes **Nullessence**, whatever its tier and
whether awake or not, which decomposes into every tier's mix, and each one it makes counts against your team's
**Lead**, whether you keep it, void it or decompose it.

Put **Quintessence** into C.O.D., by hand or from a hopper or a pipe on any side: each one cancels a fixed amount and
raises your Lead; it takes only as many as your Lead still needs, and only in its last stand. How Quintessence is
made, by Voidworks or at the Hermetic Lab, is told on the [last chapter](last_chapter.md)'s page. C.O.D. starts
slow, and speeds up, a little behind, as your Quintessence keeps pace with it, up to a ceiling; it slows back down
when you stall, so your team gains on it only by going faster than its ceiling. Your Lead never falls further than
the margin below zero, and the last stand can't be lost, stopped or reset.

When your Lead reaches the margin, C.O.D. shuts down for good and stands dark, every spell at the Hermetic Lab
ends, and your team has won the pack. The bar at the top of your screen, and Jade on C.O.D., show your Lead and
how fast C.O.D. makes Nullessence: the bar is half full when you are level.

# C.O.D.

<BlockImage id="cod" scale="4" />

C.O.D. stands on every island, and it can't be broken. It makes Advanced Mechanical Essence, and nothing else.
Right-click it to open its one slot, and take the essence it holds.

**Dormant**, as it starts, it holds a stack of essence, and makes one more at a steady pace whenever it holds less
than a stack. It puts nothing out of itself.

**Awake**: your team's first decompose on a [Recycler](recycler/index.md) wakes it, and from then on it makes
essence much faster, without stopping. It pushes each one into whatever takes items beside it, such as a hopper
or a pipe. Once its slot is full, essence piles up on its top, up to a stack, unless a block stands there. Past
that, what it makes goes nowhere: a pile on top means your Recycler is falling behind.

While your team plays a challenge, C.O.D. makes essence ever faster. Each one it makes and you don't decompose
adds to **Inflation**: should Inflation reach its cap, your challenge starts over.

## The Ports

Four **Ports** stand at C.O.D.'s corners, one for each challenge's Transmuted Essence: Galvanic for energy,
Warding for protection, Vital for life and Aureate for money. They can't be broken. A Port is dark until your team
starts its challenge, lit while the challenge is played, and glows once it is won.

A challenge is won by filling its Port with its **quota** of that essence: right-click it with the essence, or
feed it from a hopper or a pipe on any side. It takes only its own essence, only while its challenge is played,
and never more than the quota, so what's left over stays where it was. The quota is 4 while C.O.D. makes
Advanced essence, and grows with each tier below. Jade on the Port shows how far your team has got.

Winning ends the challenge, gives back what its handicap took, and blocks the tier C.O.D. made: from then on it
makes the tier below, at its pace between challenges. A won challenge can't be played again.

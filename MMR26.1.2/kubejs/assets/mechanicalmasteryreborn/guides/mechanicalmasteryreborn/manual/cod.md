# C.O.D.

<BlockImage id="cod" scale="4" />

C.O.D. stands on every island and can't be broken. It makes Advanced Mechanical Essence, and nothing else.
Right-click it to open its one slot and take what it holds.

**Dormant**, as it starts, it makes essence at a steady pace until it holds a stack, and puts nothing out of
itself.

**Awake**: your team's first decompose on a [Recycler](recycler/index.md) wakes it. From then on it makes essence
much faster, without stopping, and pushes each one into whatever takes items beside it, such as a hopper or a
pipe. Once its slot is full, essence piles up on its top, up to a stack, unless a block stands there. Past that,
what it makes is lost. A pile on top means your Recycler is falling behind.

When your team starts a [challenge](challenges/index.md), C.O.D. answers with a ritual: an ominous call and a
burst of flame and void motes, seen and heard across your island. The fish on its lid then glows red for as long
as the challenge is played. A reset, a stop or a win plays no ritual.

While your team plays a challenge, C.O.D. makes essence ever faster. Each one it makes
that you don't decompose adds to **Overproduction**. When Overproduction reaches its cap, the challenge starts over:
what its Port has taken is lost, and the Transmuted Essence made for it turns stale.

## The Ports

Four **Ports** stand at C.O.D.'s corners, one for each [challenge](challenges/index.md)'s Transmuted Essence:
Galvanic for energy, Warding for protection, Vital for life and Aureate for money. They can't be broken. A Port is
dark until its challenge starts, wears its essence's colour while the challenge is played, and glows in it once
won: light blue for the Galvanic, light grey for the Warding, lime for the Vital and yellow for the Aureate.

A challenge is won by filling its Port with its **quota** of that essence: right-click the Port with it, or feed it
from a hopper or a pipe on any side. A Port takes only its own essence, only while its challenge is played, and
never more than the quota. Jade on the Port shows how far your team has got. A reset loses what was put in.

Winning blocks the tier C.O.D. made: from then on it makes the tier below. A won challenge can't be played again.

## The last stand

Once your team has won all four challenges, C.O.D. makes nothing until you start its **last stand** at the
[lab](lab.md), in the [last chapter](last_chapter.md). From then on it makes **Nullessence**, which decomposes
into a mix drawn from every tier's. Each one it makes counts against your team's **Lead**, whether you keep it,
void it or decompose it.

Put **Quintessence** into C.O.D., by hand or from a hopper or a pipe on any side. Each one cancels a fixed amount
of Nullessence and raises your Lead. C.O.D. takes only as many as your Lead still needs, and only in its last stand.
How Quintessence is made is told on the [last chapter](last_chapter.md)'s page.

C.O.D. starts slow and speeds up as your Quintessence keeps pace with it, a little behind, up to a ceiling. When
you stall, it slows back down. Your team gains on it only by going faster than its ceiling. Your Lead never falls
further than the margin below zero, and the last stand can't be lost, stopped or reset.

When your Lead reaches the margin, C.O.D. shuts down for good and stands dark, every spell at the Hermetic Lab
ends, and your team has won the pack. The bar at the top of your screen, and Jade on C.O.D., show your Lead and
how fast C.O.D. makes Nullessence. The bar is half full when you are level.

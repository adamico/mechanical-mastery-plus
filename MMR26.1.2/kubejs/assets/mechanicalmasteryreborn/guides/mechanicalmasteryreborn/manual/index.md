# Manual

How to build and work the blocks your island runs on, and how its challenges and its last chapter are played. It
holds no recipes: EMI shows what every item takes, and what every essence decomposes into.

Hold <KeyBind id="key.guideme.guide" /> over a block or an essence in your inventory to open its page here.
Press it while looking at the lab, C.O.D. or one of its Ports, which you only ever find placed, to open theirs.

- [Your island and team](island.md), what you start with and who it counts for.
- [The Recycler](recycler/index.md), built block by block, which decomposes and recycles.
- [Decomposing and recycling](decomposing.md), by hand or on a Recycler.
- [The lab](lab.md), where your team starts a challenge, stops one, and takes its refund.
- [The challenges](challenges/index.md), the four ways back up to a Transmuted Essence, and the rules they share:
  [protection](challenges/protection.md), [energy](challenges/energy.md), [life](challenges/life.md) and
  [money](challenges/money.md).
- [C.O.D.](cod.md), which makes the essence you decompose, and its Ports.
- [The Electric Defence](fe_defence.md), which guards your island on FE.
- [The Diet](diet.md), what the life challenge does to your food.
- [The last chapter](last_chapter.md), the Nether and the End, the last stand, and the two ways to Quintessence.
- [The Hermetic Lab](hermetic_lab.md), your lab awakened in the last stand, which makes Quintessence from
  Nullessence while your team bears its handicaps as spells.

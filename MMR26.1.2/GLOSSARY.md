# Mechanical Mastery Reborn (26.1.2)

The 26.1.2 pack line, the sequel to Mechanical Mastery Plus, on NeoForge. Shared terms are in the root `GLOSSARY.md`.

**Spine**:
The suite.

**Mechanical Essence**:
On this line the loop runs backwards: a team starts with endless Advanced essence from C.O.D. and decomposes it down the tiers.

**C.O.D.**:
The line's adversary. Its endless Advanced essence has inflated the economy until basic resources are scarce, and it has become a sentient AI that cannot be shut down.

**Suite**:
The 5thlayer -works mods (Groundworks, Beltworks, Pipeworks, Wireworks, Craftworks, Fieldworks, Voidworks, Gearworks), which the pack consumes as released jars, as any player would. What the pack needs from them it scripts itself; a change goes to the mod only for a bug or a workaround worth sharing.

**Showcase**:
The FactoryWorks Showcase, the suite's own sandbox pack. A separate pack that this line borrows a base of mods from, and never depends on.

**Companion mod**:
Mechanical Mastery Reborn's own mod, which holds its own blocks and machines: the lab, C.O.D. and the decomposing machine. The pack consumes it as a released jar, like the suite.
_Avoid_: pack mod, core mod

**Decompose**:
To break a Mechanical Essence down into the intermediates it is made of, on the suite's machines, as Factorio's recycler does. It is how resources enter this line.
_Avoid_: recycle, break down (Voidworks' word for its own item breakdown)

**Inflation**:
The excess C.O.D. has built up since the current challenge began: essences it has made minus essences the team has decomposed. C.O.D. makes them ever faster, with no ceiling, so it climbs whenever decomposing falls behind; voiding or storing essence doesn't lower it. When it reaches its cap before the challenge is finished, the challenge resets and the handicap stays.
_Avoid_: glut, overflow, timer

**Transmuted Essence**:
One of the new essences, each made through Theurgy at the end of one challenge, which blocks C.O.D. on the tier the team is on. They are Galvanic (energy), Warding (protection), Vital (life) and Aureate (money). Each belongs to its challenge, not to a tier.
_Avoid_: new essence

**Quintessence**:
The final essence, made in the last chapter from the four Transmuted Essences, which shuts C.O.D. down for good.

**Challenge**:
One of four questlines, each tied to one mod, started at the lab, with the handicap taken in any order. Finishing it makes a Transmuted Essence that blocks C.O.D.'s production. The handicap the player chose for it stays until the pack ends.
_Avoid_: chapter (a quest-book page), stage

**Handicap**:
What a challenge costs the team that chose it, kept for the rest of the pack: protection (more, stronger and special mobs), life (less max health), energy (generator blocks disabled) or money (less money).
_Avoid_: threat, debuff

**Lab**:
The special block where a team chooses a handicap to start a challenge, and feeds science items to research what the challenge unlocks.

**Research**:
Consuming science items in the lab to unlock a challenge's recipes for the whole team.
_Avoid_: stage, unlock (as a noun)

**Shop**:
Where essences and goods are traded for money. On this line it is a side path, and the money handicap is what makes it matter.

# Blurb# Blurb

At the end of MMP you are very rich thanks to the creation of a massive industrial machine abomination called COD. COD has amassed an almost infinite amount of the magenta mechanical essences. The result is a huge economy inflation which has rendered all the basic resources very scarce and expensive.

In order to restore balance in the world you have decided to shutdown COD, in vain… The high processing has rendered COD sentient and it’s now an advance and autonomous AI which you cannot stop with your current level of technical and mechanical knowledge.

You start searching for ancient knowledge in old tomes of alchemy and other occult sciences with the hope of transmuting the abundant magenta essences into basic resources.

COD quickly realizes your efforts and has found a way to increase the productivity of the factory.

Your role is to build new automation lines to thwart the magenta essences production and find the means of stopping COD forever.  

# Main objectives

### Process old essences

- you have an infinite amount of magenta essences
- magenta essences can be processed into yellow essences and a specific set of basic resources
- yellow into blue, blue into red
- each essence can be processed in multiple ways (options increasing with each new tier)

### Synthesize new essences

Once you have finished processing all the old mechanical essences and amassed a high amount of the basic resources you now can start the production of a new (set of ?) essence(s?) using new techniques.

## Progression

Four named quest chapters for each major content mod

Chapters can be undertaken in any order

Once a chapter is started it must be finished (all non optional quests have to be completed) before being able to start another chapter

The chapter can be reset to try another chapter instead  

Last chapter for the final essence(s)

## Vision

- Which is the general theme?
    - A quest based technologically disappointed alchemy/druid magic skyblock pack

- Which is the style?
    - high tech vs no tech
- What does it add that it is new?
    - inverse automation (from complex to simple)
    - non-linear questline
        - Energy Threat
            - Tier1: no more infinite energy (converts creative battery into finite container)
            - Tier2: reduces energy production
            - Tier3: reduces energy production
            - Tier4: no more energy production
        - tech degradation
        - defeat enemy waves
        - madness
        - hot or warm climate
    - Custom interactions
        - Ender Essence
            - Mods
                - Ender IO
                - Energized Power
                - Create
                - Pneumatic Craft
            - Progressing through the questline increases the effects of the energy threat
            - Crafting Effect: remove the energy threat
        - 
- What does it remove from the vanilla experience?
    - no ore gen
    - no dimensions
    - progression is reversed
        - start powerful and rich
        - lose more and more material resources
        - acquire metaphysical resources and lore
        - create new resources
        - get back to power in a new way

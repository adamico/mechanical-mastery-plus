# Mechanical Mastery Plus (1.19.2)

The 1.19.2 pack line, a fork of Ammonium's Mechanical Mastery. Shared terms are in the root `GLOSSARY.md`.

**Spine**:
Create and its addons, Thermal, Mekanism and Compact Crafting.

**Mechanical Essence**:
On this line the loop runs forward: the player makes essences from resources and sells them to the shop for the next tier's resources.

**Mechanical Crystal**:
An essence pressed into a crystal, one per tier. It is the form the shop buys.

**Shop**:
Where essences and goods are traded for money, with a trade permit per tier. It drives this line's progression.
_Avoid_: AdminShop (the mod that provides it)

**C.O.D.**:
The line's ending: making it completes the pack, and its awakening is the story's "to be continued".

# Hostile spawn eggs in the Shop

Reborn's Shop sells no hostile mob's spawn egg, at any stage. Its eggs are the passive farm animals' only (chicken, cow, pig, sheep), a team's first food.

## Why this is out of scope

The drops hostile eggs would give already have a place, and eggs would undercut it:

- **Before the last chapter, the Assault and Theurgy are the drop sources.** A team saves ordinary drops ahead from them (MMR26.1.2 ADR 0010); eggs would make the Assault's ordinary drops a purchase, and its late-wave mobs (blaze, enderman, breeze, evoker) worth less to fight deep for.
- **After a protection win the home gate gives the Assault's drop on demand**, so eggs unlocked on a win would add next to nothing.
- **In the last chapter the Nether's fortresses and the End's cities give blazes, wither skeletons and shulkers free** (MMR26.1.2 ADR 0012).

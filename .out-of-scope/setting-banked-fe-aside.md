# Setting banked FE aside under the energy handicap

Reborn does not freeze a team's stored FE while it plays the energy challenge. The handicap cuts only the team's generators (MMR26.1.2 ADR 0005); FE banked beforehand, in batteries, storage, gear or an FE drive, is spent as usual.

## Why this is out of scope

Banking FE ahead of the energy challenge is meant as a strategy. A team that prepares stores power and plays the challenge on it until it runs dry; a team that doesn't must get by on hand drives and whatever else is left. That choice, and the order a team picks challenges in, is part of the pack's non-linear design. The decision is recorded in MMR26.1.2 ADR 0009.
